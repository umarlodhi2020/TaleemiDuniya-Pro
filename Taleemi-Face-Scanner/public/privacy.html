Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes Our policies and procedures on the collection, use and disclosure of Your information when You use the Taleemi Face Scanner application.

Data Collection and Usage

Our app focuses on school attendance using face recognition technology. We collect and process the following data:

  • Face Biometric Data: We capture face descriptors (mathematical representations of your face) to identify students and staff for attendance. This data is stored securely and used solely for identification within the app.
  • Profile Photos: Used to display user information during the scanning process.
  • Attendance Logs: We record the time and date of attendance for educational reporting.

Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption and secure cloud storage (Firebase) to protect your data. Biometric data is stored as non-reversible mathematical descriptors, not as raw images where possible, to enhance privacy.

Camera Permission

Our app requires access to the camera to perform face scanning and identification. Camera data is processed on-device and descriptors are sent to secure servers only for verification and storage.
